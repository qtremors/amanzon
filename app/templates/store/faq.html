Orders & Delivery

Standard shipping generally takes 3-5 business days. For our exclusive collections, we offer complimentary expedited shipping (1-2 business days) to ensure your items arrive promptly and in impeccable condition. You will receive a tracking link the moment your order departs our styling facility.

We offer a complimentary 30-day return window for all items in their original, unworn condition with tags attached. A prepaid return label is included in every package for your convenience.
